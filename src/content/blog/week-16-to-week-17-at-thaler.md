## Explaining what's changing in v1.1

Over these two weeks, we published three explanatory posts covering the new LSTs, perpetual venues, and lending markets coming with v1.1.

Rather than simply listing what's new, we wanted to explain why these additions matter.

- **New LSTs:** Expanding the liquid staking tokens available within the strategy.

https://x.com/ThalerFinance/status/2106750425865310292

- **New perpetual venues:** Broadening the venues available for perpetual trading strategies.

https://x.com/ThalerFinance/status/2107492901567488182

- **New lending markets:** Expanding the lending markets the strategy can work with.

https://x.com/ThalerFinance/status/2107129766830907719

Each addition contributes to expanding the options available to Thaler's strategies. The goal is to make the changes understandable and show how they improve the product, rather than leaving users with a changelog full of unfamiliar terms.

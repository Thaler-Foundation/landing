## Introducing Yield Upfront

We're introducing **Yield Upfront**, a new way for users to access their expected yield before it is generated.

The process is straightforward:

1. Create a vault.
2. Claim its expected yield on day one.
3. Your vault locks and runs on auto-repay.
4. The yield generated by the vault settles your claim over time.

Users can claim up to 6 months of expected net yield, providing liquidity today while their deposits continue working.

## Auto-Claim: Yield on Schedule

Alongside auto-compounding, we've introduced auto-claim.

Yield is now automatically claimed every four weeks, removing the need for users to manually collect it on each cycle.

An 11% service fee on generated yield keeps the engine running.

Your yield, collected on schedule.

## Your Yield Now Earns Yield

We've introduced auto-compounding to make yield management simpler.

Instead of manually handling every payout, generated yield goes back into your vault every four weeks, allowing it to continue earning.

- Yield is automatically compounded every four weeks.
- An 11% service fee applies to generated yield.

## Responding to the Railway Security Incident

When the Railway security incident came to light, we proactively placed Thaler under maintenance and temporarily paused vault actions while we secured our infrastructure.

We rotated all keys across Vercel, Privy and Neon, while ensuring that all existing positions remained in place throughout the process.

![](/blog/blog-13/image4.png)

Once the necessary security measures were completed, we brought Thaler back online and restored vault actions.

![](/blog/blog-13/image5.png)

Security comes before availability. We'd rather temporarily pause operations and verify our infrastructure than compromise the safety of user positions.

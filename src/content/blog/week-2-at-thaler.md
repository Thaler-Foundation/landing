Last week we opened the doors. This week people walked through them, one call at a time, and the vaults started to fill.

Here is what we did.

## Vaults are stacking SOL

25 vaults are live on mainnet, holding 50 SOL between them. 15 people are behind them. Several opened more than one, to put more of their SOL to work.

That is the part we did not want to rush, and did not. Last week there were 2 vaults and 4 SOL, and both were ours. This week the capital is mostly other people's, and it arrived the slow way, through onboarding calls rather than an open door.

Here is where it stands, straight from the dashboard.

![](/blog/blog-3/image1.jpeg)

2.21 SOL in total yield, earned to date across the vaults. That is the real number, the one already in the vaults rather than projected forward.

The dashboard also shows a predicted APY of 16.50%. Read that for what it is: a projection, the rate the strategy is pacing toward, not a promise and not money in hand. It sits inside the band we gave you last week, high single digits to the low 20s, which remains the honest version of what to expect.

The newest vaults are only days old. There is nothing to read into them yet, and we are not going to build a story around numbers this young. We open them, we point at them, and we let them run in the open. Same as last week.

---

## One call at a time, as promised

Last week we said we would onboard each person on a call. Slower, does not scale, doing it anyway. We did exactly that. 15 people, 15 conversations, every vault set up with someone from the team on the line.

The whole point of doing it by hand was to watch where the flow got rough and fix it. It did two things for us.

We revamped the dashboard, driven directly by what users told us on the calls. A lot of that is already shipped, and more is in progress.

And we made the backend faster. Creating a new vault used to take around 3 minutes. It is now closer to 1.5 minutes. You feel that difference at exactly the moment it matters, when you are setting up the thing you are about to put real money into.

We will keep doing it this way for as long as it is the right thing to do, and not a moment longer than that.

We closed Aggressive, Optimistic and YOLO. Markets can't support those risk levels right now, and we won't chase yield the market isn't paying for.

Safe, Conservative and Balanced stay open:

- **Safe**: built to protect what you put in.
- **Conservative**: a touch more yield, the same calm.
- **Balanced**: more yield, still hedged.

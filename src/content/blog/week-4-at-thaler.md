## The biggest stress test yet

This week gave the vaults their hardest test since launch. One part of the strategy recorded its worst reading since inception, driven by the highest recorded spike in SOL borrow rates on Kamino, closing July 8 at a negative \~28% APY (highest peak temporarily reached \~38%).

![](/blog/blog-5/image1.jpeg)

Our safety systems did exactly what they are built to do. They stepped in automatically, cut the drag across the fleet by roughly \~87%, and kept every vault's looping spread in a safe range the entire time, with the lowest recorded at negative \~2.85%. No manual intervention, no missed response, no surprises.

Recovery is already underway. Between the system's own repair mechanics and gains captured elsewhere in the strategy during the same stretch, the vaults are not just healing, they are coming out of it ahead. Full recovery from the last 5 days of stress will take a matter of days.

This was the biggest stress test we have encountered, and it passed successfully. That is what **built for the long run** is actually supposed to mean. We will not walk through the mechanics here, but the short version is the one that matters: when the market moved hard against one part of the vault, the system caught it, safety triggers fired in a matter of ticks, and the vault held.

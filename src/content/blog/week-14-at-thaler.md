## Building for the Stock Market

We are also participating in **Stocklana**, a Solana hackathon focused on building products around tokenized stocks. Thaler has always been built around delta-neutral strategies, and for Stocklana, we are applying the same approach to tokenized stocks, pairing the underlying asset with a hedge on Phoenix's perpetual market to reduce their directional risks.

The goal is to explore whether the same model we use for crypto-native assets can be applied to tokenized stocks on Solana, giving users a way to earn from these assets without relying entirely on their price going up.

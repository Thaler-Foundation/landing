## Closing this first test round, opening the next

We are still in private beta. What is changing is the round.

Every vault in this first round will be closed automatically. Everyone gets their deposit back and generated yield (minus the 11% service fee on the yield), plus 50% of their original vault creation fee back.

The next private beta round will run on the full stable version, with auditing planned to be done in parallel. Minimum deposits will rise to 10 SOL, and the people who will join this next round are not just testers anymore, they are early users of a final version that is going through audit.
